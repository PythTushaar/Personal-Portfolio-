Market Concentration

Bagmati and Gandaki dominate arrivals, creating a clear gateway-and-leisure tourism pattern.

Underserved West

Karnali and Sudurpashchim have high nature value but need stronger access and destination packaging.

Pilgrimage Spine

Lumbini, Janakpur, and Kathmandu form a heritage corridor with strong cross-border potential.

GIS Operations

Use layer modes, hotspots, circuits, and measurement to compare geography with tourism demand.

Geographic & Tourism Dynamics

The system shows how Nepal's tourism footprint is concentrated around Bagmati and Gandaki, while western provinces contain strong nature destinations that remain comparatively under-visited.

Switching between arrivals and accessibility helps compare tourism demand with infrastructure readiness. Hotspot and circuit overlays make the dashboard useful for planning, not only display.
